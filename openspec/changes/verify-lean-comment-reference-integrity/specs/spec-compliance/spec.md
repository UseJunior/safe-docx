## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Legacy comment integrity coverage names exact boundaries

The coverage registry SHALL describe relationship-selected Transitional legacy
comment definition/reference integrity over fixed-main admitted stories and
bind claims to the vendored declarations for `comments`, `CT_Comments`,
`comment`, `CT_Comment`, `CT_TrackChange`, `commentReference`, `CT_Markup`,
`w:id`, and `ST_DecimalNumber`.

#### Scenario: Coverage is regenerated for protocol v6
- **WHEN** protocol-v6 coverage is generated
- **THEN** it SHALL cite Part 1 §§17.13.4.2, 17.13.4.5, 17.13.4.6, and
  17.18.10 only for behavior exercised by the checker
- **AND** implementation resource, path, ordering, canonicalization, and
  certificate policies SHALL remain identified as SafeDocX policy
- **AND** the existing §17.18.10 registry title and claim SHALL be generalized
  from note identifiers to bounded WordprocessingML decimal identifiers

### Requirement: Coverage preserves explicit comment exclusions

Coverage SHALL distinguish unique legacy reference-to-definition resolution
from comment range topology and Microsoft extension semantics.

#### Scenario: Coverage does not overclaim
- **WHEN** generated coverage describes comments
- **THEN** it SHALL permit unique unreferenced definitions
- **AND** it SHALL exclude range start/end pairing, nesting, topology,
  author/content/rendering semantics, threaded-comment extensions, full OPC,
  content types, full schema validation, Strict namespaces, and rebuild

### Requirement: Comment proof targets have exact axiom and dependency audits

The seven byte-native typed theorem targets pinned in the design SHALL each
have an empty normalized axiom set and no `String`, `String.toUTF8`,
`Lean.Json`, `IO`, production, or LeanSpike dependency. The five separately
named executable refinement bridges SHALL each have exactly
`[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` and the exact signature and recursive
executable closure pinned in the design. The production refinement target SHALL
also have exactly
`[propext, Classical.choice,
Quot.sound]` and no LeanSpike engine or residual dependency. The complete
normalized union SHALL remain the repository's existing exact six-name
allowlist.

#### Scenario: Proof audits reject model-only success
- **WHEN** signatures, imports, normalized `#print axioms`, and recursive
  constant dependencies are audited
- **THEN** all seven semantic declarations and the production refinement
  declaration SHALL match the exact design signatures
- **AND** all five executable refinement declarations SHALL match their exact
  signatures and prove concrete UTF-8/relationship/realization/source/result
  operations refine typed values
- **AND** independent semantics SHALL not depend on production selector,
  scanner, integrity, response-builder, encoder, sorter, coalescer, budget,
  terminal, parser recomputation, extraction recomputation, or success bits
- **AND** independent protocol projection drift witnesses SHALL fail for every
  inherited or new field, value, array order, coalescing, budget, and terminal
  mutation, including omitted or changed `commentIntegrityIssues`
- **AND** the selector-to-realization bridge SHALL audit metadata admission,
  immutable snapshot evidence, retained extraction, retained Comments parse,
  canonical admitted-source construction, retained source-scan reuse, and
  retained one-call comment-scan dependencies explicitly
- **AND** the source bridge SHALL derive `TypedPriorSourceAdmission` from
  retained selection issues, retained note-scan presence, the concrete
  semantic crossing, and retained note-evaluation completeness rather than a
  caller-supplied admission Boolean
- **AND** semantic negative witnesses SHALL reject absent selected evidence,
  substituted retained scan evidence, and any package-view bytes/index mismatch
  with the corresponding retained package record
- **AND** the typed index relation SHALL reject unsafe or duplicate names,
  malformed or mismatched local headers, overlapping/out-of-package spans,
  incomplete entry metadata, and zero or multiple selected-path entries
- **AND** the typed index relation SHALL parse and bind the classic EOCD and
  every complete central record directly from retained package bytes, with
  closed CRC/size/offset/count/name and missing-EOCD mutations
- **AND** EOCD candidate discovery SHALL be index-independent and SHALL reject
  a closed archive containing two structurally valid EOF-aligned candidates
  before ZIP64-marker and index binding
- **AND** complete safe-name archives SHALL witness the exact 256-byte raw ZIP
  filename acceptance boundary and 257-byte rejection boundary before decoding
- **AND** the strict decoder mutation matrix SHALL cover every Comments issue
  code with a wrong sentinel or boundary, zero count, wrong source, wrong
  ordinal space, wrong side status, and forbidden extra fields
- **AND** that matrix SHALL be driven by an explicit canonical 40-code table,
  delete every required code-specific extra, mutate every extra's type and
  bound, reject count two for every non-coalescing code, and test both adjacent
  values around each exact 4,096 sentinel
- **AND** the audit SHALL not impose a blanket production-constant denial on a
  theorem whose signature intentionally names its executable bridge
- **AND** observed and allowed project executable closures SHALL both be
  recursively expanded from declaration-module provenance and compared for
  exact equality, with missing-required and forbidden-extra self-tests

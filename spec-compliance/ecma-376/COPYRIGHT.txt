ECMA-376 Office Open XML File Formats — vendored source publications
=====================================================================

The four ZIP files under `source-artifacts/`, and every standard publication
and supporting file contained in those unchanged ZIPs, are unmodified copies
distributed by Ecma International as part of the ECMA-376, 5th Edition Office
Open XML standard:

    Part 1 (Fundamentals and Markup Language Reference), December 2016
    Part 2 (Open Packaging Conventions),                December 2021
    Part 3 (Markup Compatibility and Extensibility),    December 2015
    Part 4 (Transitional Migration Features),           December 2016

The canonical downloads live at:

    https://ecma-international.org/publications-and-standards/standards/ecma-376/

Those ZIPs are reproduced verbatim and have not been edited or recompressed.
Their SHA-256 identities are recorded in `source-artifacts/SHA256SUMS` and the
artifact manifest. This COPYRIGHT.txt is preserved alongside them per Ecma
International's text copyright policy. The authentic copies remain those
published by Ecma.

The files under `schemas/` are separately stored, unmodified derived copies of
the normative XSD and informative RELAX NG files extracted from the Part 1,
Part 2, and Part 4 source ZIPs. The coverage check verifies the XSD copies byte
for byte against their owning nested archives. Part 3 contains prose and has no
schema-copy directory in this repository.
